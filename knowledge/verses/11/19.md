# Chapter 11, Verse 19

Part of [Vishwaroopa Darshana Yoga](/chapters/11.md).

## Sanskrit

अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्य

मनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम्।

पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रम्

स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम्।।11.19।।

## Transliteration

स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम् || 19||

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I behold You as having no beginning, middle, or end; Your might is infinite. You are endowed with an innumerable number of arms. The sun and moon are Your eyes, and Your mouth emits burning fire. With Your own radiance, You are warning the entire universe.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: I see You as having no beginning, middle, or end, possessing infinite valor, with innumerable arms, the sun and moon as eyes, a mouth like a blazing fire, and heating up this universe with Your own brilliance.
- *Swami Sivananda*: I see You without beginning, middle, or end, infinite in power, with endless arms, the sun and moon as Your eyes, the burning fire Your mouth, heating the entire universe with Your radiance.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: I observe You with no beginning, no middle, and no end; having infinite creative power and infinite arms; with the moon and the sun as Your eyes and the blazing fire as Your mouth; and scorching this universe with Your radiance.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Without beginning, without middle, and without end, infinite in power, with arms all-embracing, the sun and moon as Your eyes, Your face beaming with the fire of sacrifice, flooding the whole universe with light.

## Themes

Realizing your own limits when faced with an infinite power that has no beginning, middle, or end.

**Keywords:** infinite, endless, boundless, powerful, overwhelming, fearsome, unstoppable, vast, limitless, intense, terrifying, all-seeing
