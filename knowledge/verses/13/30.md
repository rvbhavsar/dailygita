# Chapter 13, Verse 30

Part of [Ksetra Ksetrajna Vibhaaga Yoga](/chapters/13.md).

## Sanskrit

प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वशः।यः पश्यति तथाऽऽत्मानमकर्तारं स पश्यति।।13.30।।

## Transliteration

prakṛityaiva cha karmāṇi kriyamāṇāni sarvaśhaḥ
yaḥ paśhyati tathātmānam akartāraṁ sa paśhyati

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: He who sees that all acts are done universally by Prakrti alone, and likewise that the self is not the doer, he indeed sees.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: And he who sees actions being done in various ways by Nature itself, and also the Self as the non-agent, he sees.
- *Swami Sivananda*: He sees, who sees that all actions are performed solely by Nature and that the Self is without action.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Whoever views all actions as being performed (or all objects as being created) by the Material Cause itself, and at the same time views their own Self as a non-performer (or non-creator) - they view properly.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: He who understands that it is only the Law of Nature that brings action to fruition, and that the Self never acts, alone knows the Truth.

## Word meanings

prakṛityā—by material nature; eva—truly; cha—also; karmāṇi—actions; kriyamāṇāni—are performed; sarvaśhaḥ—all; yaḥ—who; paśhyati—see; tathā—also; ātmānam—(embodied) soul; akartāram—actionless; saḥ—they; paśhyati—see
