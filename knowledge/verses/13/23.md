# Chapter 13, Verse 23

Part of [Ksetra Ksetrajna Vibhaaga Yoga](/chapters/13.md).

## Sanskrit

उपद्रष्टाऽनुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः।परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः।।13.23।।

## Transliteration

upadraṣhṭānumantā cha bhartā bhoktā maheśhvaraḥ
paramātmeti chāpy ukto dehe ’smin puruṣhaḥ paraḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The self in the body is called the spectator, approver, supporter, experiencer, great lord, and supreme self.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: He who is the Witness, the Permitter, the Sustainer, the Experiencer, the great Lord, and who is also spoken of as the transcendental Self, is the supreme Person in this body.
- *Swami Sivananda*: The Supreme Soul in this body is also called the observer, the permitter, the sustainer, the enjoyer, the great Lord, and the Supreme Self.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: The Supreme Soul in this corporeal body is called the Spectator, the Assenter, the Supporter, the Experiencer, the Mighty Lord, and also the Supreme Self.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Thus, in the body of man dwells the Supreme God; He who sees, permits, upholds, and enjoys is the Highest God and the Highest Self.

## Word meanings

upadraṣhṭā—the witness; anumantā—the permitter; cha—and; bhartā—the supporter; bhoktā—the transcendental enjoyer; mahā-īśhvaraḥ—the ultimate controller; parama-ātmā—Superme Soul; iti—that; cha api—and also; uktaḥ—is said; dehe—within the body; asmin—this; puruṣhaḥ paraḥ—the Supreme Lord

## Themes

There is a calm inner observer within you that watches, allows, and sustains you beyond daily ups and downs.

**Keywords:** inner witness, higher self, watching thoughts, calm inside, grounded, inner strength, observer, support, presence, self awareness
