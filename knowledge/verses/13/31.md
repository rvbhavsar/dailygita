# Chapter 13, Verse 31

Part of [Ksetra Ksetrajna Vibhaaga Yoga](/chapters/13.md).

## Sanskrit

यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपश्यति।तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म सम्पद्यते तदा।।13.31।।

## Transliteration

yadā bhūta-pṛithag-bhāvam eka-stham anupaśhyati
tata eva cha vistāraṁ brahma sampadyate tadā

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: When he perceives the independent modes of existence of all beings centered in one, and their expansion from it alone, then he attains Brahman.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: When one realizes that the state of diversity of living things is rooted in the One, and that their manifestation also comes from That, then one becomes identified with Brahman.
- *Swami Sivananda*: When a person sees all beings as resting in the One and emanating from the One alone, they then become Brahman.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: When he perceives the mutual difference of beings as abiding in One, and its expansion from That alone, at that time he becomes the Brahman.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: He who sees the diverse forms of life all rooted in One and growing forth from Him, shall indeed find the Absolute.

## Word meanings

yadā—when; bhūta—living entities; pṛithak-bhāvam—diverse variety; eka-stham—situated in the same place; anupaśhyati—see; tataḥ—thereafter; eva—indeed; cha—and; vistāram—born from; brahma—Brahman; sampadyate—(they) attain; tadā—then

## Themes

Realizing that all the diversity of life is rooted in one shared source and emerges from it.

**Keywords:** diversity, unity, oneness, interconnected, belonging, togetherness, wholeness, inclusion, differences, common ground, humanity, connection
