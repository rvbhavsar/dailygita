# Chapter 13, Verse 20

Part of [Ksetra Ksetrajna Vibhaaga Yoga](/chapters/13.md).

## Sanskrit

प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव विद्ध्यनादी उभावपि।विकारांश्च गुणांश्चैव विद्धि प्रकृतिसंभवान्।।13.20।।

## Transliteration

prakṛitiṁ puruṣhaṁ chaiva viddhy anādī ubhāv api
vikārānśh cha guṇānśh chaiva viddhi prakṛiti-sambhavān

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Know that both Prakṛti and the Self (Puruṣa) are without beginning; know that all modifications and the attributes are born from Prakṛti.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Know both Nature and the individual soul to be verily without beginning; know the modifications as well as the qualities to be born of Nature.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Know that Nature (matter) and the Spirit are both beginningless, and know also that all modifications and qualities are born from Nature.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Both the Material Cause and the Soul are beginningless; you should know this. You should also know that the modifications and strands are born from the Material Cause.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Know further that Nature and God have no beginning, and that differences in character and quality originate from Nature only.

## Word meanings

prakṛitim—material nature; puruṣham—the individual souls; cha—and; eva—indeed; viddhi—know; anādī—beginningless; ubhau—both; api—and; vikārān—transformations (of the body); cha—also; guṇān—the three modes of nature; cha—and; eva—indeed; viddhi—know; prakṛiti—material energy; sambhavān—produced by

## Themes

Distinguishing between your lasting inner self and the temporary changes produced by material nature.

**Keywords:** nature, self, body, mind, emotions, habits, change, permanent, eternal, patterns, thoughts, moods, matter, awareness
