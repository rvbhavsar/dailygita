# Chapter 13, Verse 25

Part of [Ksetra Ksetrajna Vibhaaga Yoga](/chapters/13.md).

## Sanskrit

ध्यानेनात्मनि पश्यन्ति केचिदात्मानमात्मना।अन्ये सांख्येन योगेन कर्मयोगेन चापरे।।13.25।।

## Transliteration

dhyānenātmani paśhyanti kechid ātmānam ātmanā
anye sānkhyena yogena karma-yogena chāpare

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Some perceive the Self within the self (body) through meditation of the mind, others through Sankhya Yoga, and still others through Karma Yoga.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Some realize the Self in their intellect through meditation, others through Sankhya-Yoga, and still others through Karma-Yoga, with the help of the internal organ.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Some behold the Self within themselves through meditation, others through the Yoga of knowledge, and still others through the Yoga of action.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: However, some yogis perceive the Self as the Self within the self through meditation, others through knowledge-yoga, and still others through action-yoga.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Some realize the Supreme by meditating, with its aid, on the Self within; others by pure reason; and still others by right action.

## Word meanings

dhyānena—through meditation; ātmani—within one’s heart; paśhyanti—percieve; kechit—some; ātmānam—the Supreme soul; ātmanā—by the mind; anye—others; sānkhyena—through cultivation of knowledge; yogena—the yog system; karma-yogena—union with God with through path of action; cha—and; apare—others
