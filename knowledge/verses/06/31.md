# Chapter 6, Verse 31

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः।

सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते।।6.31।।

## Transliteration

sarva-bhūta-sthitaṁ yo māṁ bhajatyekatvam āsthitaḥ
sarvathā vartamāno ’pi sa yogī mayi vartate

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The yogi who, fixed in oneness, worships Me dwelling in all beings—he abides in Me, no matter how he may live.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: That yogi, who, being established in unity, adores Me as existing in all things, exists in Me—in whatever condition he may be.
- *Swami Sivananda*: He who, being established in unity, worships Me, who dwells in all beings, that yogi abides in Me, whatever their mode of living may be.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: He who is firmly established in the oneness of Me, experiences Me immanent in all beings—that man of Yoga is never stained, no matter what stage he may be in.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The sage who realizes the unity of life and who worships Me in all beings, lives in Me, no matter what their lot may be.

## Word meanings

sarva-bhūta-sthitam—situated in all beings; yaḥ—who; mām—me; bhajati—worships; ekatvam—in unity; āsthitaḥ—established; sarvathā—in all kinds of; varta-mānaḥ—remain; api—although; saḥ—he; yogī—a yogi; mayi—in me; vartate—dwells
