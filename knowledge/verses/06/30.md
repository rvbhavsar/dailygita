# Chapter 6, Verse 30

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति।

तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति।।6.30।।

## Transliteration

yo māṁ paśhyati sarvatra sarvaṁ cha mayi paśhyati
tasyāhaṁ na praṇaśhyāmi sa cha me na praṇaśhyati

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: To him who sees Me in every self and every self in Me—I am not lost to him, nor is he lost to Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: One who sees Me in everything and sees all things in Me—I do not lose sight of him, and he also is not lost to My vision.
- *Swami Sivananda*: He who sees Me everywhere and sees everything in Me, never becomes separated from Me, nor do I from him.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: He who observes Me in all and observes all in Me—for him, I am not lost, and he too is not lost for me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: He who sees Me in everything and everything in Me, I shall never forsake him, nor shall he lose Me.
