# Chapter 6, Verse 26

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम्।

ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत्।।6.26।।

## Transliteration

yato yato niśhcharati manaśh chañchalam asthiram
tatas tato niyamyaitad ātmanyeva vaśhaṁ nayet

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Wherever the fickle and unsteady mind wanders, one should subdue it and then bring it back under the control of the self alone.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: The yogi should bring this mind under the subjugation of the Self itself, restraining it from all those causes that cause the restless, unsteady mind to wander away.
- *Swami Sivananda*: From whatever cause the restless and unsteady mind wanders away, let him restrain it from that and bring it under the control of the Self alone.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: By whatever things the shaky and unsteady mind strays, let him restrain it from those things and bring it back under the control of the Self alone.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: When the volatile and wavering mind wanders, let him restrain it and bring it back to its allegiance to the Self.

## Word meanings

yataḥ yataḥ—whenever and wherever; niśhcharati—wanders; manaḥ—the mind; chañchalam—restless; asthiram—unsteady; tataḥ tataḥ—from there; niyamya—having restrained; etat—this; ātmani—on God; eva—certainly; vaśham—control; nayet—should bring

## Themes

Bringing a restless and wandering mind back under steady control again and again.

**Keywords:** restless mind, wandering thoughts, mental chatter, bringing back focus, taming the mind, unsettled, distractible, calming, grounding, regaining control
