# Chapter 6, Verse 45

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः।

अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम्।।6.45।।

## Transliteration

prayatnād yatamānas tu yogī sanśhuddha-kilbiṣhaḥ
aneka-janma-sansiddhas tato yāti parāṁ gatim

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: But the yogi, striving earnestly, cleansed of all his stains and perfected through many births, reaches the supreme state.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: However, the yogi, applying himself assiduously, becoming purified from sin and attaining perfection through many births, they achieve the highest Goal.
- *Swami Sivananda*: But the Yogi who strives assiduously, purified of sins and perfected gradually over many births, reaches the highest goal.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: After that, the assiduous man of Yoga, having his sins completely cleansed and being perfected through many births, reaches the Supreme Goal.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Then, after many lives of earnest striving and absolution of sins, the student of spirituality attains perfection and reaches the Supreme.

## Word meanings

prayatnāt—with great effort; yatamānaḥ—endeavoring; tu—and; yogī—a yogi; sanśhuddha—purified; kilbiṣhaḥ—from material desires; aneka—after many, many; janma—births; sansiddhaḥ—attain perfection; tataḥ—then; yāti—attains; parām—the highest; gatim—path

## Themes

Sticking with sincere, consistent effort over a long time to grow and eventually reach your highest potential.

**Keywords:** keep going, steady effort, long haul, gradual progress, never giving up, patience, daily practice, slow growth, persistence, showing up, improvement over time
