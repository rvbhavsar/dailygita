# Chapter 6, Verse 22

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः।

यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते।।6.22।।

## Transliteration

yaṁ labdhvā chāparaṁ lābhaṁ manyate nādhikaṁ tataḥ
yasmin sthito na duḥkhena guruṇāpi vichālyate

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Having gained it, one holds that there is no greater gain; being established in it, one is not moved even by the heaviest sorrow.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Obtaining which one does not consider any other acquisition to be superior, and being established in which one is not perturbed even by great sorrow;
- *Swami Sivananda*: Having obtained it, he thinks there is no other gain superior to it; established in it, he is not moved even by heavy sorrow.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: And having attained it, he does not think of any other gain as superior to that; being established in it, he is not shaken much by misery, however powerful it may be.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Finding That, he will realize that there is no possession so precious. And once established there, no calamity can disturb him.

## Word meanings

yam—which; labdhvā—having gained; cha—and; aparam—any other; lābham—gain; manyate—considers; na—not; adhikam—greater; tataḥ—than that; yasmin—in which; sthitaḥ—being situated; na—never; duḥkhena—by sorrow; guruṇā—(by) the greatest; api—even; vichālyate—is shaken

## Themes

Gaining an inner stability so strong that no loss or hardship can easily shake you.

**Keywords:** resilience, unshaken, hardship, suffering, strength, inner gain, true wealth, steady, courage, enduring peace
