# Chapter 6, Verse 39

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

एतन्मे संशयं कृष्ण छेत्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः।

त्वदन्यः संशयस्यास्य छेत्ता न ह्युपपद्यते।।6.39।।

## Transliteration

etan me sanśhayaṁ kṛiṣhṇa chhettum arhasyaśheṣhataḥ
tvad-anyaḥ sanśhayasyāsya chhettā na hyupapadyate

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: O Krsna, you should altogether remove this doubt of mine, for there is no other remover of this doubt than you.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Krsna, you should totally eradicate this doubt of mine, for none other than yourself can be the dispeller of this doubt!
- *Swami Sivananda*: O Krishna, please completely dispel this doubt of mine, for it is not possible for anyone but You to do so.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Please dispel this doubt of mine completely. But for You, O Krsna, no one else is capable of eradicating this doubt.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: My Lord! You are worthy to solve this doubt once and for all; there is no one else competent to do so, save Yourself.

## Word meanings

etat—this; me—my; sanśhayam—doubt; kṛiṣhṇa—Krishna; chhettum—to dispel; arhasi—you can; aśheṣhataḥ—completely; tvat—than you; anyaḥ—other; sanśhayasya—of doubt; asya—this; chhettā—a dispeller; na—never; hi—certainly; upapadyate—is fit

## Themes

Turning to a trusted guide to completely clear a deep, persistent doubt.

**Keywords:** doubt, confusion, clarity, answers, guidance, help, unsure, stuck, questioning, worried, seeking, teacher
