# Chapter 6, Verse 13

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचलं स्थिरः।

संप्रेक्ष्य नासिकाग्रं स्वं दिशश्चानवलोकयन्।।6.13।।

## Transliteration

samaṁ kāya-śhiro-grīvaṁ dhārayann achalaṁ sthiraḥ
samprekṣhya nāsikāgraṁ svaṁ diśhaśh chānavalokayan

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Holding the trunk, head, and neck erect, motionless and steady, gazing at the tip of the nose and not looking in any direction;
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Holding the body, head, and neck erect and still, being steady, looking at the tip of his own nose and not looking around.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Let him firmly hold his body, head, and neck erect and still, gazing at the tip of his nose without looking around.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Holding the body, head, and neck erect and motionless; remaining firm; looking properly at one's own nose-tip; and not looking in different directions.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Let him hold his body, head, and neck erect, motionless and steady; let him look fixedly at the tip of his nose, turning neither to the right nor to the left.

## Word meanings

samam—straight; kāya—body; śhiraḥ—head; grīvam—neck; dhārayan—holding; achalam—unmoving; sthiraḥ—still; samprekṣhya—gazing; nāsika-agram—at the tip of the nose; svam—own; diśhaḥ—directions; cha—and; anavalokayan—not looking
