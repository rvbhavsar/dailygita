# Chapter 6, Verse 42

Part of [Dhyana Yoga](/chapters/06.md).

## Sanskrit

अथवा योगिनामेव कुले भवति धीमताम्।

एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम्।।6.42।।

## Transliteration

atha vā yoginām eva kule bhavati dhīmatām
etad dhi durlabhataraṁ loke janma yad īdṛiśham

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Or he is born into a family of wise yogis. But such a birth in this world is rare.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Or, he is born in the family of wise yogis. Such a birth is indeed more difficult to come by in the world.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Or he is born in a family of even the wisest of yogis; verily, such a birth is very difficult to obtain in this world.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Or, he is born nowhere else but in the family of the intelligent men of Yoga; for, this birth is more difficult to obtain in the world.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Or, he may be born in the family of wise sages, though such a birth is indeed very difficult to obtain.

## Word meanings

atha vā—else; yoginām—of those endowed with divine wisdom; eva—certainly; kule—in the family; bhavati—take birth; dhī-matām—of the wise; etat—this; hi—certainly; durlabha-taram—very rare; loke—in this world; janma—birth; yat—which; īdṛiśham—like this

## Themes

The rare good fortune of being born into a wise and disciplined family that helps you grow.

**Keywords:** rare, wise, family, mentors, guidance, blessed, opportunity, growth, environment, upbringing, support, lucky
