# Chapter 17, Verse 6

Part of [Sraddhatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/17.md).

## Sanskrit

कर्षयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः।मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान्।।17.6।।

## Transliteration

karṣhayantaḥ śharīra-sthaṁ bhūta-grāmam achetasaḥ
māṁ chaivāntaḥ śharīra-sthaṁ tān viddhy āsura-niśhchayān

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: These foolish men, who torture the group of elements in their bodies and Me, who dwell within the body—know them to be demoniacal in their resolves.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: (And those who,) being non-discriminating, torture all the organs in the body as well as even Me who reside in the body—know them to be possessed of demoniacal conviction.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Know thou these to be of demonical resolves, senselessly torturing all the elements in the body and Me who dwell in the body.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Those who unintelligently emaciate the conglomeration of elements in their physical body and emaciate Me, dwelling within the physical body—know them to be of a demoniac resolve.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: They are ignorant and torment the organs of the body, harassing Me who lives within. Know that they are devoted to evil.

## Word meanings

karṣhayantaḥ—torment; śharīra-stham—within the body; bhūta-grāmam—elements of the body; achetasaḥ—senseless; mām—me; cha—and; eva—even; antaḥ—within; śharīra-stham—dwelling in the body; tān—them; viddhi—know; āsura-niśhchayān—of demoniacal resolves
