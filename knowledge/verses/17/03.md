# Chapter 17, Verse 3

Part of [Sraddhatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/17.md).

## Sanskrit

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत।श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः।।17.3।।

## Transliteration

sattvānurūpā sarvasya śhraddhā bhavati bhārata
śhraddhā-mayo ‘yaṁ puruṣho yo yach-chhraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: O Arjuna, the faith of everyone is in accordance with their inner organ (Antahkarana). Each person consists of faith; whatever their faith is, that is who they are.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, the faith of all beings is in accordance with their minds. This person is composed of faith as the dominant factor. He is indeed what his faith is.
- *Swami Sivananda*: The faith of each is in accordance with their nature, O Arjuna. People consist of their faith; as a person's faith is, so are they.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Corresponding to one's own sattva, O descendant of Bharata, everybody has faith. The person predominantly consists of faith. Whatever one has faith in, that he becomes certainly.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Every person's faith conforms to their nature. By nature, they are full of faith. They are, in fact, what their faith makes them.

## Word meanings

sattva-anurūpā—conforming to the nature of one’s mind; sarvasya—all; śhraddhā—faith; bhavati—is; bhārata—Arjun, the scion of Bharat; śhraddhāmayaḥ—possessing faith; ayam—that; puruṣhaḥ—human being; yaḥ—who; yat-śhraddhaḥ—whatever the nature of their faith; saḥ—their; eva—verily; saḥ—they

## Themes

Understanding that you become the person your deepest faith and convictions shape you to be.

**Keywords:** belief, identity, mindset, becomes what you believe, self image, values, inner faith, character, who i am, heart, trust
