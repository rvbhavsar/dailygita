# Chapter 17, Verse 16

Part of [Sraddhatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/17.md).

## Sanskrit

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिग्रहः।भावसंशुद्धिरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते।।17.16।।

## Transliteration

manaḥ-prasādaḥ saumyatvaṁ maunam ātma-vinigrahaḥ
bhāva-sanśhuddhir ity etat tapo mānasam uchyate

## Translations

- *Swami Sivananda*: Serenity of mind, good-heartedness, self-control, and purity of nature—this is called mental austerity.
- *Swami Adidevananda*: Serenity of mind, benevolence, silence, self-control, and purity of mind—these are known as austerity of the mind.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Tranquility of mind, gentleness, reticence, withdrawal of the mind, purity of heart—these are what are called mental austerities.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: The serenity of mind, the stillness, the taciturnity, the self-control, the purity of thought—all this is called mental austerity.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Serenity, kindness, silence, self-control, and purity—these are the austerities of the mind.

## Word meanings

manaḥ-prasādaḥ—serenity of thought; saumyatvam—gentleness; maunam—silence; ātma-vinigrahaḥ—self-control; bhāva-sanśhuddhiḥ—purity of purpose; iti—thus; etat—these; tapaḥ—austerity; mānasam—of the mind; uchyate—are declared as
