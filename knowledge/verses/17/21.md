# Chapter 17, Verse 21

Part of [Sraddhatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/17.md).

## Sanskrit

यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः।दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम्।।17.21।।

## Transliteration

yat tu pratyupakārārthaṁ phalam uddiśhya vā punaḥ
dīyate cha parikliṣhṭaṁ tad dānaṁ rājasaṁ smṛitam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: But that which is given as a consideration for something received or in expectation of a future reward, or grudgingly, is called a Rajasika gift.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: But the gift which is given expecting reciprocation, or with a desire for its result, and grudgingly, is considered to be born of rajas.
- *Swami Sivananda*: And, that gift which is given with the intention of receiving something in return, or expecting a reward, or begrudgingly, is considered to be Rajasic.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: But what is given in return for a favor, or with the expectation of a reward, and which is done with much agitation—that gift is said to be of the Rajas.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: That which is given for the sake of the results it will produce, or with the hope of recompense, or grudgingly—that may truly be said to be the outcome of passion.

## Word meanings

yat—which; tu—but; prati-upakāra-artham—with the hope of a return; phalam—reward; uddiśhya—expectation; vā—or; punaḥ—again; dīyate—is given; cha—and; parikliṣhṭam—reluctantly; tat—that; dānam—charity; rājasam—in the mode of passion; smṛitam—is said to be
