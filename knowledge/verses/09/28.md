# Chapter 9, Verse 28

Part of [Raja Vidya Yoga](/chapters/09.md).

## Sanskrit

शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः।

संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि।।9.28।।

## Transliteration

śhubhāśhubha-phalair evaṁ mokṣhyase karma-bandhanaiḥ
sannyāsa-yoga-yuktātmā vimukto mām upaiṣhyasi

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Thus equipped in mind with the Yoga of renunciation, you will free yourself from the bonds of Karma, productive of both auspicious and inauspicious fruits. Thus liberated, you will come to Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Thus, you will become free from bondage in the form of actions which are productive of good and bad results. Having your mind imbued with the yoga of renunciation and becoming free, you will attain Me.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Thus, you shall be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with the mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, and liberated, you shall come to Me.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Thus, you shall be freed from the good and evil results which are the action-bonds. Having your innate nature immersed in the Yoga of renunciation and, thus, being fully liberated, you shall attain Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: So, your action will have no result, either good or bad; but, through the spirit of renunciation, you will come to Me and be free.

## Word meanings

śhubha aśhubha phalaiḥ—from good and bad results; evam—thus; mokṣhyase—you shall be freed; karma—work; bandhanaiḥ—from the bondage; sanyāsa-yoga—renunciation of selfishness; yukta-ātmā—having the mind attached to me; vimuktaḥ—liberated; mām—to me; upaiṣhyasi—you shall reach

## Themes

Learning to act fully while letting go of attachment to good or bad results so you feel free.

**Keywords:** freedom, letting go, guilt, burden, consequences, release, detachment, surrender, peace, unburdened, weight
