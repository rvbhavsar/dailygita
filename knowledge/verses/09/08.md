# Chapter 9, Verse 8

Part of [Raja Vidya Yoga](/chapters/09.md).

## Sanskrit

प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः।

भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात्।।9.8।।

## Transliteration

prakṛitiṁ svām avaṣhṭabhya visṛijāmi punaḥ punaḥ
bhūta-grāmam imaṁ kṛitsnam avaśhaṁ prakṛiter vaśhāt

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Controlling the Prakṛti, which is My own, I send forth again and again this multitude of beings, helpless under the sway of Prakṛti.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Keeping My own prakṛti under control, I project forth again and again this multitude of beings, which are powerless due to the influence of their own nature.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Animating My Nature, I again and again send forth all this multitude of beings, helpless under the force of Nature.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Taking hold of My own nature, I again and again send forth this entire host of beings, which is powerless under the control of My nature.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: With the help of Nature, I again and again pour forth the whole multitude of beings, whether they will or not, for they are ruled by My will.

## Word meanings

prakṛitim—the material energy; svām—my own; avaṣhṭabhya—presiding over; visṛijāmi—generate; punaḥ punaḥ—again and again; bhūta-grāmam—myriad forms; imam—these; kṛitsnam—all; avaśham—beyond their control; prakṛiteḥ—nature; vaśhāt—force

## Themes

Feeling stuck in repeating loops and powerless because your own ingrained habits keep driving you.

**Keywords:** stuck, powerless, trapped, helpless, repeating, cycle, habits, overwhelmed, out of control, can't change
