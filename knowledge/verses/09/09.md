# Chapter 9, Verse 9

Part of [Raja Vidya Yoga](/chapters/09.md).

## Sanskrit

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय।

उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु।।9.9।।

## Transliteration

na cha māṁ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya
udāsīna-vad āsīnam asaktaṁ teṣhu karmasu

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: But these actions do not bind Me, O Arjuna, for I remain detached from them, as if I were unconcerned.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Dhananjaya, those actions do not bind Me, for I remain unconcerned and unattached to them.
- *Swami Sivananda*: These acts do not bind Me, O Arjuna, sitting as one indifferent, unattached to those acts.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O Dhananjaya! These acts do not bind me, remaining as if unconcerned and unattached in these actions.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: But these acts of mine do not bind me; I remain outside and unattached.

## Word meanings

na—none; cha—as; mām—me; tāni—those; karmāṇi—actions; nibadhnanti—bind; dhanañjaya—Arjun, conqueror of wealth; udāsīna-vat—as neutral; āsīnam—situated; asaktam—detached; teṣhu—those; karmasu—actions

## Themes

Doing your necessary work fully while staying detached so the actions and outcomes do not bind or drain you.

**Keywords:** detached, unattached, letting go, not clinging, free from results, doing without worry, calm action, unbothered, uninvolved
