# Chapter 9, Verse 4

Part of [Raja Vidya Yoga](/chapters/09.md).

## Sanskrit

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना।

मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः।।9.4।।

## Transliteration

mayā tatam idaṁ sarvaṁ jagad avyakta-mūrtinā
mat-sthāni sarva-bhūtāni na chāhaṁ teṣhvavasthitaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Sivananda*: All of this world is pervaded by Me in My unmanifest aspect; all beings exist within Me, but I do not dwell within them.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: This whole world is pervaded by Me in My unmanifest form. All beings exist in Me, but I am not contained within them!
- *Swami Adidevananda*: This entire universe is pervaded by Me, in an unmanifest form. All beings abide in Me, but I do not abide in them.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: This entire universe is pervaded by Me, having an unmanifest form; all beings exist in Me, and I do not exist in them.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The whole world is pervaded by Me, yet My form is not visible. All living beings have their being in Me, yet I am not confined by them.

## Word meanings

mayā—by me; tatam—pervaded; idam—this; sarvam—entire; jagat—cosmic manifestation; avyakta-mūrtinā—the unmanifested form; mat-sthāni—in me; sarva-bhūtāni—all living beings; na—not; cha—and; aham—I; teṣhu—in them; avasthitaḥ—dwell

## Themes

The comfort of sensing an unseen connection that holds all life and keeps you from feeling alone.

**Keywords:** connected, belonging, unseen, supported, held, part of whole, not alone, bigger picture, interwoven, presence, included, everywhere
