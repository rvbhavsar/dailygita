# Chapter 14, Verse 16

Part of [Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/14.md).

## Sanskrit

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम्।रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम्।।14.16।।

## Transliteration

karmaṇaḥ sukṛitasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṁ nirmalaṁ phalam
rajasas tu phalaṁ duḥkham ajñānaṁ tamasaḥ phalam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: It is said that the fruit of a good deed is pure and of the nature of Sattva; the fruit of Rajas is pain; and the fruit of Tamas is ignorance.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: They say that the result of good work is pure and is born of Sattva. But the result of Rajas is sorrow; the result of Tamas is ignorance.
- *Swami Sivananda*: They say that the fruit of good action is Sattvic and pure; indeed, the fruit of Rajas is pain, and the fruit of Tamas is ignorance.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: It is said that the fruit of good action is spotless and of the Sattva nature; whereas the fruit of Rajas is pain, and the fruit of Tamas is ignorance.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: They say the fruit of a meritorious action is spotless and pure; the outcome of passion is misery, and of ignorance, darkness.

## Word meanings

karmaṇaḥ—of action; su-kṛitasya—pure; āhuḥ—is said; sāttvikam—mode of goodness; nirmalam—pure; phalam—result; rajasaḥ—mode of passion; tu—indeed; phalam—result; duḥkham—pain; ajñānam—ignorance; tamasaḥ—mode of ignorance; phalam—result

## Themes

Seeing that peaceful actions bring peace, driven actions bring pain, and lazy actions bring more confusion.
