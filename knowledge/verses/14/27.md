# Chapter 14, Verse 27

Part of [Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/14.md).

## Sanskrit

ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाऽहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च।शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च।।14.27।।

## Transliteration

brahmaṇo hi pratiṣhṭhāham amṛitasyāvyayasya cha
śhāśhvatasya cha dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya cha

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: For I am the ground of Brahman, the immortal, immutable, and eternal Dharma, and perfect bliss.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: For I am the Abode of Brahman—the indestructible, immutable, eternal, Dharma, and absolute Bliss.
- *Swami Sivananda*: For I am the abode of Brahman, the immortal, immutable, and everlasting Dharma, and absolute bliss.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: 'I' am the support for the immortal and changeless Brahman and for its eternal attribute, the unalloyed happiness.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: For I am the home of the spirit, the continual source of immortality, eternal righteousness, and infinite joy.

## Word meanings

brahmaṇaḥ—of Brahman; hi—only; pratiṣhṭhā—the basis; aham—I; amṛitasya—of the immortal; avyayasya—of the imperishable; cha—and; śhāśhvatasya—of the eternal; cha—and; dharmasya—of the dharma; sukhasya—of bliss; aikāntikasya—unending; cha—and

## Themes

Searching for a steady inner foundation that gives lasting happiness and peace beyond temporary changes.

**Keywords:** lasting happiness, true peace, inner support, unshakable foundation, eternal joy, security, contentment, fulfillment, belonging, wholeness, calm, hope
