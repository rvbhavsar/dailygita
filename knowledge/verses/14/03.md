# Chapter 14, Verse 3

Part of [Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/14.md).

## Sanskrit

मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन् गर्भं दधाम्यहम्।संभवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत।।14.3।।

## Transliteration

mama yonir mahad brahma tasmin garbhaṁ dadhāmy aham
sambhavaḥ sarva-bhūtānāṁ tato bhavati bhārata

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: My womb is the great Brahman (i.e. Prakṛti). In it, I lay the germ. From that, O Arjuna, comes the birth of all beings.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: My womb is the great sustainer. In it, I place the seed. From that, O scion of the Bharata dynasty, all things are born.
- *Swami Sivananda*: My womb is the great Brahma; in it I place the germ; thence, O Arjuna, is the birth of all beings.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: The mighty Brahman is a womb for Me; and in it I lay seed; from which is the birth of all beings, O descendant of Bharata!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The eternal cosmos is My womb, in which I plant the seed from which all beings are born, O prince!

## Word meanings

mama—my; yoniḥ—womb; mahat brahma—the total material substance, prakṛiti; tasmin—in that; garbham—womb; dadhāmi—impregnate; aham—I; sambhavaḥ—birth; sarva-bhūtānām—of all living beings; tataḥ—thereby; bhavati—becomes; bhārata—Arjun, the son of Bharat;

## Themes

Understanding that all living beings share a common origin and are deeply connected.

**Keywords:** origin, source, creation, birth, belonging, unity, existence, beginning, life, connected, oneness, purpose
