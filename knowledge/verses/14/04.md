# Chapter 14, Verse 4

Part of [Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/14.md).

## Sanskrit

सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः।तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता।।14.4।।

## Transliteration

sarva-yoniṣhu kaunteya mūrtayaḥ sambhavanti yāḥ
tāsāṁ brahma mahad yonir ahaṁ bīja-pradaḥ pitā

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Whatever forms are produced in any womb, O Arjuna, the Prakṛti is their great womb, and I am the sowing father.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O son of Kunti, whatever forms are born from all the wombs, of them the great sustainer is the womb; I am the father who deposits the seed.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Whatever forms are produced, O Arjuna, in any womb whatsoever, the great Brahma is their womb, and I am the seed-giving father.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O son of Kunti! Whatever manifestations spring up in all the wombs, the mighty Brahman is the womb, and I am the father who sows the seed.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: O illustrious son of Kunti! Through whatever wombs men are born, it is the Spirit itself that conceives, and I am their father.

## Word meanings

sarva—all; yoniṣhu—species of life; kaunteya—Arjun, the son of Kunti; mūrtayaḥ—forms; sambhavanti—are produced; yāḥ—which; tāsām—of all of them; brahma-mahat—great material nature; yoniḥ—womb; aham—I; bīja-pradaḥ—seed-giving; pitā—Father

## Themes

Recognizing a shared parental source that makes all forms of life part of one family.

**Keywords:** family, parent, mother, father, belonging, birth, creation, unity, connected, origin, children, everyone, oneness, source
