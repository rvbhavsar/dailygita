# Chapter 14, Verse 13

Part of [Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/14.md).

## Sanskrit

अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च।तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन।।14.13।।

## Transliteration

aprakāśho ’pravṛittiśh cha pramādo moha eva cha
tamasy etāni jāyante vivṛiddhe kuru-nandana

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Non-illumination, inactivity, negligence, and even delusion—these arise, O Arjuna, when Tamas prevails.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O descendant of the Kuru dynasty, when tamas predominates, these come into being without exception: non-discrimination, inactivity, inadvertence, and delusion.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Darkness, inertia, carelessness, and delusion—these arise when Tamas is predominant, O Arjuna.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Absence of mental illumination, lack of exertion, negligence, and mere delusion—these are born when Tamas predominates, O darling of the Kurus!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Darkness, stagnation, folly, and infatuation are the results of the dominance of ignorance, O joy of the Kuru clan!

## Word meanings

aprakāśhaḥ—nescience; apravṛittiḥ—inertia; cha—and; pramādaḥ—negligence; mohaḥ—delusion; eva—indeed; cha—also; tamasi—mode of ignorance; etāni—these; jāyante—manifest; vivṛiddhe—when dominates; kuru-nandana—the joy of the Kurus, Arjun

## Themes

Understanding how inertia, confusion and carelessness cloud judgment and keep you stuck.

**Keywords:** lethargic, inactive, confused, foggy, careless, deluded, stuck, numb, procrastinating, unmotivated, unclear, lazy
