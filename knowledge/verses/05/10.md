# Chapter 5, Verse 10

Part of [Karma Sanyasa Yoga](/chapters/05.md).

## Sanskrit

ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः।

लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा।।5.10।।

## Transliteration

brahmaṇyādhāya karmāṇi saṅgaṁ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ
lipyate na sa pāpena padma-patram ivāmbhasā

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: He who acts without attachment, reposing all actions on Brahman (Prakṛti), is untouched by evil, just as a lotus leaf is untouched by water.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: One who acts by dedicating their actions to Brahman and by renouncing attachment, they do not become polluted by sin, just as a lotus leaf is not by water.
- *Swami Sivananda*: He who does actions, offering them to Brahman and abandoning attachment, is not tainted by sin, just as a lotus leaf is not tainted by water.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Who performs actions by offering them to the Brahman and giving up attachment—they are not stained by sin, just as a lotus leaf is not stained by water.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: He who dedicates his actions to the Spirit, without any personal attachment to them, is no more tainted by sin than the water lily is wetted by water.

## Word meanings

brahmaṇi—to God; ādhāya—dedicating; karmāṇi—all actions; saṅgam—attachment; tyaktvā—abandoning; karoti—performs; yaḥ—who; lipyate—is affected; na—never; saḥ—that person; pāpena—by sin; padma-patram—a lotus leaf; iva—like; ambhasā—by water

## Themes

Working with dedication to a higher purpose and without clinging to results keeps you light and unstained like a lotus leaf above water.

**Keywords:** dedication, nonattachment, unstained, guilt free, letting results go, purpose beyond self, clean conscience, doing without clinging, light, lotus like
