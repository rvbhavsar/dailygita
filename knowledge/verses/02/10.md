# Chapter 2, Verse 10

Part of [Sankhya Yoga](/chapters/02.md).

## Sanskrit

तमुवाच हृषीकेशः प्रहसन्निव भारत।

सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये विषीदन्तमिदं वचः।।2.10।।

## Transliteration

tam-uvācha hṛiṣhīkeśhaḥ prahasanniva bhārata
senayorubhayor-madhye viṣhīdantam-idaṁ vachaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: O King, to him who was thus sorrowing between the two armies, Sri Krishna spoke the following words, as if smiling (by way of ridicule).
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O descendant of Bharata, Hrsikesa, mocking as it were, said these words to him who was sorrowing between the two armies:
- *Swami Sivananda*: To him who was despondent in the midst of the two armies, Krishna, smiling, O Bharata, spoke these words.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O descendant of Bharata, Hrsikesa, as if smiling, spoke to him who was sinking in despondency between two armies.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Thereupon, the Lord, with a gracious smile, addressed him who was so much depressed in the midst of the two armies.

## Word meanings

tam—to him; uvācha—said; hṛiṣhīkeśhaḥ—Shree Krishna, the master of mind and senses; prahasan—smilingly; iva—as if; bhārata—Dhritarashtra, descendant of Bharat; senayoḥ—of the armies; ubhayoḥ—of both; madhye—in the midst of; viṣhīdantam—to the grief-stricken; idam—this; vachaḥ—words

## Themes

Being in deep sorrow and confusion while awaiting compassionate guidance from a steady friend.

**Keywords:** sorrowful, stuck, torn, lonely, overwhelmed, breakdown, confusion, support, guidance, friendship, listening, compassion
