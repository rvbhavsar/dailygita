# Chapter 2, Verse 23

Part of [Sankhya Yoga](/chapters/02.md).

## Sanskrit

नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावकः।

न चैनं क्लेदयन्त्यापो न शोषयति मारुतः।।2.23।।

## Transliteration

nainaṁ chhindanti śhastrāṇi nainaṁ dahati pāvakaḥ
na chainaṁ kledayantyāpo na śhoṣhayati mārutaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Weapons cannot cleave It, fire cannot burn It, waters cannot wet It, and wind cannot dry It.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Weapons cannot cut It, fire cannot burn It, water cannot moisten It, and air cannot dry It.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Weapons cannot cut it, fire cannot burn it, water cannot wet it, wind cannot dry it.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Weapons cannot cut This; fire cannot burn This; water cannot make This wet; and the wind cannot make This dry.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Weapons cannot cleave it, fire cannot burn it, water cannot drench it, and wind cannot dry it.

## Word meanings

na—not; enam—this soul; chhindanti—shred; śhastrāṇi—weapons; na—nor; enam—this soul; dahati—burns; pāvakaḥ—fire; na—not; cha—and; enam—this soul; kledayanti—moisten; āpaḥ—water; na—nor; śhoṣhayati—dry; mārutaḥ—wind
