# Chapter 2, Verse 27

Part of [Sankhya Yoga](/chapters/02.md).

## Sanskrit

जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च।

तस्मादपरिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि।।2.27।।

## Transliteration

jātasya hi dhruvo mṛityur dhruvaṁ janma mṛitasya cha
tasmād aparihārye ’rthe na tvaṁ śhochitum arhasi

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: For, death is certain for the born, and rebirth is certain for the dead; therefore, you should not grieve for what is inevitable.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: For the death of anyone born is certain, and the rebirth of the dead is certain. Therefore, you should not grieve over an inevitable fact.
- *Swami Sivananda*: For the born, death is certain, and for the dead, birth is certain; therefore, you should not grieve over the inevitable.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Death is certain indeed for what is born; and birth is certain for the dead. Therefore, you should not lament over something that is unavoidable.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: For death is certain for that which is born, just as birth is certain for that which is dead. Therefore, do not grieve for what is inevitable.

## Word meanings

jātasya—for one who has been born; hi—for; dhruvaḥ—certain; mṛityuḥ—death; dhruvam—certain; janma—birth; mṛitasya—for the dead; cha—and; tasmāt—therefore; aparihārye arthe—in this inevitable situation; na—not; tvam—you; śhochitum—lament; arhasi—befitting
