# Chapter 2, Verse 59

Part of [Sankhya Yoga](/chapters/02.md).

## Sanskrit

विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः।

रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते।।2.59।।

## Transliteration

viṣhayā vinivartante nirāhārasya dehinaḥ
rasa-varjaṁ raso ’pyasya paraṁ dṛiṣhṭvā nivartate

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The objects of the senses, except for the pleasure they bring, turn away from the abstinent dweller in the body. Even the pleasure turns away from him when the Self, which is supreme over the senses, is seen.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: The objects recede from an abstinent person, except for the taste for them. Even this taste falls away after realization of the Absolute.
- *Swami Sivananda*: The objects of the senses turn away from the abstinent man, leaving the longing behind; but his longing also turns away upon seeing the Supreme.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Leaving their taste behind, the sense-objects retreat from the embodied one who abstains from food; his taste too disappears when he beholds the Supreme.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The objects of sense turn away from him who is abstinent. Even the taste for them is lost in him who has seen the Truth.

## Word meanings

viṣhayāḥ—objects for senses; vinivartante—restrain; nirāhārasya—practicing self restraint; dehinaḥ—for the embodied; rasa-varjam—cessation of taste; rasaḥ—taste; api—however; asya—person’s; param—the Supreme; dṛiṣhṭvā—on realization; nivartate—ceases to be
