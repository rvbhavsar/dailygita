# Chapter 2, Verse 40

Part of [Sankhya Yoga](/chapters/02.md).

## Sanskrit

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते।

स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्।।2.40।।

## Transliteration

nehābhikrama-nāśho ’sti pratyavāyo na vidyate
svalpam apyasya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Here, there is no loss of effort, nor any accrual of evil. Even a little of this Dharma (called Karma Yoga) protects one from the great fear.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Here, there is no waste of effort; nor is there any harm. Even a little of this righteousness saves one from great fear.
- *Swami Sivananda*: In this, there is no loss of effort, nor is there any harm produced, nor any transgression. Even a little of this knowledge protects one from great fear.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Here, there is no loss due to transgression, and there is no contrary downward course (sin); even a little of this righteous thing can save one from great danger.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: On this path, endeavour is never wasted nor repressed. Even a little practice of it protects one from great danger.

## Word meanings

na—not; iha—in this; abhikrama—efforts; nāśhaḥ—loss; asti—there is; pratyavāyaḥ—adverse result; na—not; vidyate—is; su-alpam—a little; api—even; asya—of this; dharmasya—occupation; trāyate—saves; mahataḥ—from great; bhayāt—danger

## Themes

Even tiny sincere efforts are never wasted and can protect you from major harm.

**Keywords:** effort, progress, small, beginner, failure, safety, protection, fear, wasted, starting, hope, courage
