# Chapter 10, Verse 41

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा।

तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसंभवम्।।10.41।।

## Transliteration

yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śhrīmad ūrjitam eva vā
tat tad evāvagachchha tvaṁ mama tejo ’nśha-sambhavam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Whatever being is possessed of power, or splendour, or energy, know that as coming from a fragment of My power.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Whatever object [all living beings] is verily endowed with majesty, possessed of prosperity, or is energetic, you know for certain each of them as having a part of My power as its source.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Whatever being there is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be a manifestation of a part of My splendor.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Whatever being exists with manifesting power, beauty, and vigor, be sure that it is born only from a bit of My illumination.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Whatever is glorious, excellent, beautiful, and mighty, be assured that it comes from a fragment of My splendor.

## Word meanings

yat yat—whatever; vibhūtimat—opulent; sattvam—being; śhrī-mat—beautiful; ūrjitam—glorious; eva—also; vā—or; tat tat—all that; eva—only; avagachchha—know; tvam—you; mama—my; tejaḥ-anśha-sambhavam—splendor; anśha—a part; sambhavam—born of

## Themes

Seeing any form of real greatness, beauty or power as a glimpse of a greater universal energy.

**Keywords:** greatness, excellence, brilliance, success, power, inspiring, talented, admirable, prosperous, energetic, gifted, extraordinary
