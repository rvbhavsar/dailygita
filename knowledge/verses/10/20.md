# Chapter 10, Verse 20

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः।

अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च।।10.20।।

## Transliteration

aham ātmā guḍākeśha sarva-bhūtāśhaya-sthitaḥ
aham ādiśh cha madhyaṁ cha bhūtānām anta eva cha

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I am the Self, O Arjuna, dwelling in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all beings.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Gudakesa, I am the Self residing in the hearts of all beings, and I am the beginning, middle, and end of all beings.
- *Swami Sivananda*: I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all beings.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O conqueror of sleep! I am the Soul residing in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and the very end of all beings.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: O Arjuna! I am the Self, seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the life, and the end of them all.

## Word meanings

aham—I; ātmā—soul; guḍākeśha—Arjun, the conqueror of sleep; sarva-bhūta—of all living entities; āśhaya-sthitaḥ—seated in the heart; aham—I; ādiḥ—the beginning; cha—and; madhyam—middle; cha—and; bhūtānām—of all beings; antaḥ—end; eva—even; cha—also

## Themes

Sensing a common presence in all hearts fosters empathy and a sense of belonging to a larger whole.

**Keywords:** connection, oneness, unity, belonging, togetherness, shared, compassion, inner, heart, everyone, beginning, ending, always
