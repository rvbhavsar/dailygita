# Chapter 10, Verse 39

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन।

न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम्।।10.39।।

## Transliteration

yach chāpi sarva-bhūtānāṁ bījaṁ tad aham arjuna
na tad asti vinā yat syān mayā bhūtaṁ charācharam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I am also that which is the seed of all beings, O Arjuna. Nothing that moves or does not move exists without Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Moreover, O Arjuna, I am the seed of all beings. There is nothing, moving or non-moving, that can exist without Me.
- *Swami Sivananda*: And whatever is the seed of all beings, that too am I, O Arjuna; there is no being, be it moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Further, O Arjuna, I am the seed of all beings; there is no being, whether moving or non-moving, that can exist without Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: I am the seed of all beings, O Arjuna! No creature, moving or unmoving, can live without Me.

## Word meanings

yat—which; cha—and; api—also; sarva-bhūtānām—of all living beings; bījam—generating seed; tat—that; aham—I; arjuna—Arjun; na—not; tat—that; asti—is; vinā—without; yat—which; syāt—may exist; mayā—me; bhūtam—creature; chara-acharam—moving and nonmoving

## Themes

Realizing that all beings share a common source and nothing exists independently.

**Keywords:** connected, belonging, source, origin, seed, existence, unity, interdependence, included, universal, dependent, togetherness
