# Chapter 10, Verse 5

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः।

भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः।।10.5।।

## Transliteration

ahiṁsā   samatā  tuṣṭis tapo dānaṁ   yaśo    'yaśaḥ  bhavanti    bhāvā   bhūtānāṁ    matta   eva pṛthag-vidhāḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Non-violence, reality, cheerfulness, austerity, beneficence, fame, and infamy—these different qualities of beings arise from Me alone.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Non-injury, equanimity, satisfaction, austerity, charity, fame, and infamy—these different dispositions of beings spring from Me alone.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Non-injury, equanimity, contentment, austerity, beneficence, fame, and ill-fame—these different qualities of beings arise from Me alone.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: [Also], non-injury, equanimity, contentment, austerity, charity, repute, and ill-repute—all these diverse dispositions of beings emanate from none other than Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Harmlessness, equanimity, contentment, austerity, beneficence, fame, and failure—all these characteristics of beings spring from Me alone.

## Word meanings

ahiṁsā—nonviolence;    samatā—equilibrium; tuṣṭiḥ—satisfaction;    tapaḥ—penance;  dānam—charity;  yaśaḥ—fame; ayaśaḥ—infamy;  bhavanti—become;    bhāvāḥ—natures;     bhūtānām—of living  entities;   mattaḥ—from Me; eva—certainly;  pṛthakvidhāḥ—differently    arranged.
