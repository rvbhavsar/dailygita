# Chapter 10, Verse 14

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां वदसि केशव।

न हि ते भगवन् व्यक्ितं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः।।10.14।।

## Transliteration

sarvam etad ṛitaṁ manye yan māṁ vadasi keśhava
na hi te bhagavan vyaktiṁ vidur devā na dānavāḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I deem as true all that you have said to me, O Krishna. Truly, O Lord, neither the gods nor the demons know your manifestation.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Kesava, I accept as true all that You have told me. Certainly, O Lord, neither the gods nor the demons can comprehend Your glory.
- *Swami Sivananda*: I believe all that You have said to me to be true, O Krishna; indeed, O blessed Lord! Neither the gods nor the demons know Your manifestation (origin).
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: What You tell me, I take all to be true, O Kesava! For, O Bhagavat, neither the gods nor the great sages know Your manifestation.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: I believe in what You have said, my Lord! For neither the godly nor the godless comprehend Your manifestation.

## Word meanings

sarvam—everything; etat—this; ṛitam—truth; manye—I accept; yat—which; mām—me; vadasi—you tell; keśhava—Shree Krishna, the killer of the demon named Keshi; na—neither; hi—verily; te—your; bhagavan—the Supreme Lord; vyaktim—personality; viduḥ—can understand; devāḥ—the celestial gods; na—nor; dānavāḥ—the demons

## Themes

Learning to trust a deeper truth even when its origin is beyond human understanding.

**Keywords:** belief, trust, faith, surrender, mystery, wonder, acceptance, humility, awe, reverence, truth, unknown
