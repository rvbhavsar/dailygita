# Chapter 10, Verse 16

Part of [Vibhooti Yoga](/chapters/10.md).

## Sanskrit

वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः।

याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठसि।।10.16।।

## Transliteration

vaktum arhasyaśheṣheṇa divyā hyātma-vibhūtayaḥ
yābhir vibhūtibhir lokān imāṁs tvaṁ vyāpya tiṣhṭhasi

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: You should tell Me without reserve Your divine manifestations where You abide, pervading all these worlds.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Be pleased to speak in full of Your own divine manifestations, through which You exist pervading these worlds.
- *Swami Sivananda*: You should indeed tell, without reserve, of your divine glories by which you exist, pervading all these worlds. (No one else can do so.)
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: You alone are capable of fully declaring the auspicious manifesting powers of Yours, through which manifesting power You remain pervading these worlds.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Please tell me about all of Your glorious manifestations, through which You pervade the world.

## Word meanings

vaktum—to describe; arhasi—please do; aśheṣheṇa—completely; divyāḥ—divine; hi—indeed; ātma—your own; vibhūtayaḥ—opulences; yābhiḥ—by which; vibhūtibhiḥ—opulences; lokān—all worlds; imān—these; tvam—you; vyāpya—pervade; tiṣhṭhasi—reside;
