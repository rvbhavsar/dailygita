# Chapter 3, Verse 18

Part of [Karma Yoga](/chapters/03.md).

## Sanskrit

नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन।

न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्िचदर्थव्यपाश्रयः।।3.18।।

## Transliteration

naiva tasya kṛitenārtho nākṛiteneha kaśhchana
na chāsya sarva-bhūteṣhu kaśhchid artha-vyapāśhrayaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: He has no purpose to gain from work done or left undone, nor does he have to rely on any end.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: For him, there is no concern here at all with performing action, nor any with non-performance. Moreover, he has no dependence on any object to serve any purpose.
- *Swami Sivananda*: For him, there is no interest whatsoever in what is done or not done; nor does he depend on any being for any purpose.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: No purpose is served for him by what he has done or by what he has not done. For him, there is hardly any dependence on any purpose among all beings.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: He has nothing to gain from the performance or non-performance of action; his welfare does not depend on any contribution that an earthly creature can make.

## Word meanings

na—not; eva—indeed; tasya—his; kṛitena—by discharge of duty; arthaḥ—gain; na—not; akṛitena—without discharge of duty; iha—here; kaśhchana—whatsoever; na—never; cha—and; asya—of that person; sarva-bhūteṣhu—among all living beings; kaśhchit—any; artha—necessity; vyapāśhrayaḥ—to depend upon
