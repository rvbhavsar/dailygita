# Chapter 3, Verse 1

Part of [Karma Yoga](/chapters/03.md).

## Sanskrit

अर्जुन उवाच

ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन।

तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव।।3.1।।

## Transliteration

arjuna uvācha
jyāyasī chet karmaṇas te matā buddhir janārdana
tat kiṁ karmaṇi ghore māṁ niyojayasi keśhava

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Arjuna said, "O Krsna, if you deem knowledge to be superior to action, why then do you urge me to engage in this terrible deed?"
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Arjuna said, "O Janardana (Krishna), if it is Your opinion that wisdom is superior to action, why do You urge me to take such horrible action, O Kesava?"
- *Swami Sivananda*: Arjuna said: If Thou thinkest that knowledge is superior to action, O Krishna, why then, O Kesava, doest Thou ask me to engage in this terrible action?
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Arjuna said, "O Janardana, if you hold knowledge to be superior to action, then why do you engage me in such terrible action, O Kesava?"
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Arjuna questioned: My Lord! If wisdom is superior to action, why do you advise me to engage in this terrible fight?

## Word meanings

arjunaḥ uvācha—Arjun said; jyāyasī—superior; chet—if; karmaṇaḥ—than fruitive action; te—by you; matā—is considered; buddhiḥ—intellect; janārdana—he who looks after the public, Krishna; tat—then; kim—why; karmaṇi—action; ghore—terrible; mām—me; niyojayasi—do you engage; keśhava—Krishna, the killer of the demon named Keshi;

## Themes

Wrestling with whether to think more or act now when both paths feel contradictory and urgent.

**Keywords:** confusion, stuck, dilemma, indecision, thinking, doing, action, wisdom, guidance, conflict, hesitate, unclear, torn, choice
