# Chapter 3, Verse 23

Part of [Karma Yoga](/chapters/03.md).

## Sanskrit

यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः।

मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः।।3.23।।

## Transliteration

yadi hyahaṁ na varteyaṁ jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣhyāḥ pārtha sarvaśhaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: If I did not continue to work unweariedly, O Arjuna, people would follow my path.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: For, O Partha, if at any time I do not continue vigilantly in action, people will follow My path in every way.
- *Swami Sivananda*: For, should I not ever engage myself in action, unwearied, people would in every way follow my path, O Arjuna.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: For, if I were ever not to work unwearied, all men would follow My path, O son of Prtha!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Were I not to act without ceasing, O prince, people would be inclined to do the same.

## Word meanings

yadi—if; hi—certainly; aham—I; na—not; varteyam—thus engage; jātu—ever; karmaṇi—in the performance of prescribed duties; atandritaḥ—carefully; mama—my; vartma—path; anuvartante—follow; manuṣhyāḥ—all men; pārtha—Arjun, the son of Pritha; sarvaśhaḥ—in all respects

## Themes

Understanding that your actions set the standard others will follow.

**Keywords:** role model, influence, example, responsibility, being watched, accountability, leadership, vigilance, followed, team
