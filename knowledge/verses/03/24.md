# Chapter 3, Verse 24

Part of [Karma Yoga](/chapters/03.md).

## Sanskrit

उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम्।

सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः।।3.24।।

## Transliteration

utsīdeyur ime lokā na kuryāṁ karma ched aham
sankarasya cha kartā syām upahanyām imāḥ prajāḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: If I do not do the work, these people will be lost; and I will be causing chaos in life and thereby ruining all these people.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: These worlds would be ruined if I did not perform action. And I shall become the agent of intermingling (of castes), and shall be destroying these beings.
- *Swami Sivananda*: These worlds would perish if I did not perform action; I would be the author of confusion of castes and destruction of these beings.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: These worlds would perish if I were not to perform action; and I would be the cause of confusion; I would destroy these people.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: And if I were to refrain from action, the human race would be ruined; I would lead the world to chaos, and destruction would follow.

## Word meanings

utsīdeyuḥ—would perish; ime—all these; lokāḥ—worlds; na—not; kuryām—I perform; karma—prescribed duties; chet—if; aham—I; sankarasya—of uncultured population; cha—and; kartā—responsible; syām—would be; upahanyām—would destroy; imāḥ—all these; prajāḥ—living entities

## Themes

Recognizing that avoiding your responsibilities can cause wider breakdown and harm.

**Keywords:** inaction, consequences, responsibility, chaos, breakdown, duty, accountability, society, neglect, harm, interdependence
