# Chapter 8, Verse 5

Part of [Akshara Brahma Yoga](/chapters/08.md).

## Sanskrit

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम्।

यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः।।8.5।।

## Transliteration

anta-kāle cha mām eva smaran muktvā kalevaram
yaḥ prayāti sa mad-bhāvaṁ yāti nāstyatra sanśhayaḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: And at the time of death, anyone who departs, giving up the body while thinking of Me alone, attains My state; there is no doubt about this.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: And at the time of death, anyone who departs, giving up the body while thinking of Me alone, attains My state; there is no doubt about this.
- *Swami Sivananda*: And whoever, leaving their body, goes forth remembering Me alone at the time of death, they will attain My Being; there is no doubt about this.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Whosoever, at the time of death, remembering Me alone, sets forth, abandoning their body behind, they attain My being. There is no doubt about it.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Whosoever, at the time of death, thinks only of Me and, thinking thus, leaves the body and goes forth, will assuredly know Me.

## Word meanings

anta-kāle—at the time of death; cha—and; mām—me; eva—alone; smaran—remembering; muktvā—relinquish; kalevaram—the body; yaḥ—who; prayāti—goes; saḥ—he; mat-bhāvam—Godlike nature; yāti—achieves; na—no; asti—there is; atra—here; sanśhayaḥ—doubt

## Themes

Learning that the thoughts and devotion you hold at the end of life determine your ultimate journey.

**Keywords:** death, dying, last thoughts, remembrance, devotion, faith, surrender, letting go, afterlife, liberation, peace, final focus
