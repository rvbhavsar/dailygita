# Chapter 8, Verse 7

Part of [Akshara Brahma Yoga](/chapters/08.md).

## Sanskrit

तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च।

मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम्।।8.7।।

## Transliteration

tasmāt sarveṣhu kāleṣhu mām anusmara yudhya cha
mayyarpita-mano-buddhir mām evaiṣhyasyasanśhayam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Therefore, think of Me at all times and fight; there is no doubt that by dedicating your mind and intellect to Me, you will attain only Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Therefore, think of Me at all times and fight; there is no doubt that by dedicating your mind and intellect to Me, you will attain only Me.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Therefore, at all times, remember Me only and fight. With your mind and intellect fixed on Me, you will undoubtedly come to Me alone.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Therefore, at all times keep Me in your mind and fight; then, having your mind and intellect dedicated to Me, you will undoubtedly attain Me alone.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Therefore, always meditate on Me and fight; if your mind and reason are fixed on Me, you will surely come to Me.

## Word meanings

tasmāt—therefore; sarveṣhu—in all; kāleṣhu—times; mām—me; anusmara—remember; yudhya—fight; cha—and; mayi—to me; arpita—surrender; manaḥ—mind; buddhiḥ—intellect; mām—to me; eva—surely; eṣhyasi—you shall attain; asanśhayaḥ—without a doubt

## Themes

Staying connected to what matters most while you keep doing your necessary work.

**Keywords:** purpose, action, work, duty, remember, centered, dedicated, heart, mind, balance, busy, meaning
