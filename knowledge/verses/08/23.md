# Chapter 8, Verse 23

Part of [Akshara Brahma Yoga](/chapters/08.md).

## Sanskrit

यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः।

प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ।।8.23।।

## Transliteration

yatra kāle tvanāvṛittim āvṛittiṁ chaiva yoginaḥ
prayātā yānti taṁ kālaṁ vakṣhyāmi bharatarṣhabha

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: O best of the Bharata dynasty, I shall now speak of that time by which the yogis depart and attain the State of Non-return, and also the State of Return.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O best of the Bharata dynasty, I shall now speak of that time by which the yogis depart and attain the State of Non-return, and also the State of Return.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Now I will tell you, O chief of the Bharatas, the times of departure at which the Yogis will return or not return.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Departing at what times the Yogis attain the non-return or the return only—those times I shall declare to you, O chief of the Bharatas!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Now, I will tell you, O Arjuna, the times at which, if the mystics go forth, they do not return, and at which they go forth only to return.

## Word meanings

yatra—where; kāle—time; tu—certainly; anāvṛittim—no return; āvṛittim—return; cha—and; eva—certainly; yoginaḥ—a yogi; prayātāḥ—having departed; yānti—attain; tam—that; kālam—time; vakṣhyāmi—I shall describe; bharata-ṛiṣhabha—Arjun, the best of the Bharatas;

## Themes

Understanding that the timing and direction of your life choices determine where you ultimately end up.

**Keywords:** timing, life path, crossroads, choice of direction, consequences, return or not, long term outcome, where you end up, moment of leaving, future journey
