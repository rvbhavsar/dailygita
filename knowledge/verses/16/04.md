# Chapter 16, Verse 4

Part of [Daivasura Sampad Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/16.md).

## Sanskrit

दम्भो दर्पोऽभिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च।अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ सम्पदमासुरीम्।।16.4।।

## Transliteration

dambho darpo ’bhimānaśh cha krodhaḥ pāruṣhyam eva cha
ajñānaṁ chābhijātasya pārtha sampadam āsurīm

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Pompousness, arrogance, self-conceit, wrath, rudeness, and ignorance—these, O Arjuna, belong to one who is born with a demonic destiny.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O son of Prtha, the attributes of one destined to have the demoniacal nature are religious ostentation, pride, haughtiness, self-conceit, anger, rudeness, and ignorance.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Hypocrisy, arrogance, and self-conceit, anger, harshness, and ignorance—these belong to one who is born for a demoniacal state, O Partha.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Ostentation, arrogance, pride, anger, and harshness, as well as ignorance, are characteristics of a person born of demoniac wealth, O son of Prtha!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Hypocrisy, pride, insolence, cruelty, and ignorance belong to those born of godless qualities.

## Word meanings

dambhaḥ—hypocrisy; darpaḥ—arrogance; abhimānaḥ—conceit; cha—and; krodhaḥ—anger; pāruṣhyam—harshness; eva—certainly; cha—and; ajñānam—ignorance; cha—and; abhijātasya—of those who possess; pārtha—Arjun, the son of Pritha; sampadam—qualities; āsurīm—demoniac

## Themes

Recognizing how pride, anger, rudeness, and pretense create toxic character and harm relationships.

**Keywords:** arrogance, ego, pride, anger, rudeness, fakeness, conceit, ignorance, meanness, bullying, showoff, superiority
