# Chapter 16, Verse 8

Part of [Daivasura Sampad Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/16.md).

## Sanskrit

असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम्।अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम्।।16.8।।

## Transliteration

asatyam apratiṣhṭhaṁ te jagad āhur anīśhvaram
aparaspara-sambhūtaṁ kim anyat kāma-haitukam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: They maintain: 'The universe is without truth, with no foundation, and without a Lord (Isvara). What else could exist without mutual causation? It has lust as its cause.'
- *Swami Gambirananda*: They say that the world is unreal, having no basis, and without a God. It is born of mutual union brought about by passion! What other cause could there be?
- *Swami Sivananda*: They say, "This universe is without truth, without a moral basis, without a God, brought about by mutual union, with lust as its cause; what else?"
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: They say that this world is without truth; has no basis; and has no Lord; this is not born on the basis of the mutual cause-and-effect-relation [of the things]; it has nothing [beyond] and has no cause.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: They say the universe is an accident with no purpose and no God. Life is created through sexual union, a product of lust and nothing else.

## Word meanings

asatyam—without absolute truth; apratiṣhṭham—without any basis; te—they; jagat—the world; āhuḥ—say; anīśhvaram—without a God; aparaspara—without cause; sambhūtam—created; kim—what; anyat—other; kāma-haitukam—for sexual gratification only

## Themes

Dealing with a cynical worldview that sees life as random, baseless, and without meaning or moral order.

**Keywords:** meaningless, purposeless, hopelessness, cynicism, no truth, no morals, randomness, no consequences, disbelief, emptiness, no point, worldview
