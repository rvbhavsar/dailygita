# Chapter 16, Verse 6

Part of [Daivasura Sampad Vibhaga Yoga](/chapters/16.md).

## Sanskrit

द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन् दैव आसुर एव च।दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे श्रृणु।।16.6।।

## Transliteration

dvau bhūta-sargau loke ’smin daiva āsura eva cha
daivo vistaraśhaḥ prokta āsuraṁ pārtha me śhṛiṇu

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: There are two types of beings in this world: the divine and the demoniac. The divine have been described at length. Hear from Me, O Arjuna, about the demoniac.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: In this world, there are two kinds of creation of beings: the divine and the demoniacal. The divine has been spoken of elaborately. Hear about the demoniacal from Me, O son of Prtha.
- *Swami Sivananda*: There are two types of beings in this world: the divine and the demoniacal. The divine has been described at length; hear from Me, O Arjuna, about the demoniacal.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: There are two types of creations of beings in this world: the divine and the demoniac. The divine one has been described in detail; hear now the demoniac one from Me, O son of Prtha!
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: No changes needed.

## Word meanings

dvau—two; bhūta-sargau—of created living beings; loke—in the world; asmin—this; daivaḥ—divine; āsuraḥ—demoniac; eva—certainly; cha—and; daivaḥ—the divine; vistaraśhaḥ—at great length; proktaḥ—said; āsuram—the demoniac; pārtha—Arjun, the son of Pritha; me—from me; śhṛiṇu—hear

## Themes

Recognizing that there are two broad ways of living and learning to tell the difference between uplifting and harmful mindsets.

**Keywords:** good vs evil, human nature, character types, understanding people, moral choice, good people, bad people, kindness, cruelty, values, two paths
