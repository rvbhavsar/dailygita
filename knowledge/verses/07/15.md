# Chapter 7, Verse 15

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः।

माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः।।7.15।।

## Transliteration

na māṁ duṣhkṛitino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ
māyayāpahṛita-jñānā āsuraṁ bhāvam āśhritāḥ

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The evil-doers, the foolish, the lowest of men, those persons deprived of knowledge by delusion (Maya) and those who are dominated by a demoniac nature - they do not seek refuge in Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: The foolish evildoers, who are the most depraved among men, who are deprived of their wisdom by Maya, and who resort to demonic ways, do not take refuge in Me.
- *Swami Sivananda*: The evil-doers and the deluded, who are the lowest of men, do not seek Me; those whose knowledge is destroyed by illusion follow the ways of demons.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: The deluded evil-doers, the vilest of men, who are robbed of knowledge by the trick of illusion and have taken refuge in the demonic nature—they do not resort to Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The sinner, the ignorant, the vile, deprived of spiritual perception by the glamour of illusion, and he who leads a godless life—none of them shall find me.

## Word meanings

na—not; mām—unto me; duṣhkṛitinaḥ—the evil doers; mūḍhāḥ—the ignorant; prapadyante—surrender; nara-adhamāḥ—one who lazily follows one’s lower nature; māyayā—by God’s material energy; apahṛita jñānāḥ—those with deluded intellect; āsuram—demoniac; bhāvam—nature; āśhritāḥ—surrender

## Themes

How harmful choices, confusion, and self-centered habits keep people from seeking higher guidance.

**Keywords:** bad choices, wrongdoing, foolishness, ignorance, self-deception, harmful habits, cruelty, arrogance, ego, closed mind, denial, avoiding help, resistance, lost, misguided
