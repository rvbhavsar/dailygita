# Chapter 7, Verse 4

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च।

अहङ्कार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा।।7.4।।

## Transliteration

bhūmir-āpo ’nalo vāyuḥ khaṁ mano buddhir eva cha
ahankāra itīyaṁ me bhinnā prakṛitir aṣhṭadhā

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, Manas, Buddhi, and the ego-sense—thus My Prakriti is divided into eight parts.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: This Prakrti of Mine is divided eightfold thus: earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect, and egoism.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My Nature divided eightfold.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: My nature is divided into eight parts: Earth, Water, Fire, Wind, Ether, Mind, Intellect, and Ego.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and personality—this is the eightfold division of My manifested nature.

## Word meanings

bhūmiḥ—earth; āpaḥ—water; analaḥ—fire; vāyuḥ—air; kham—space; manaḥ—mind; buddhiḥ—intellect; eva—certainly; cha—and; ahankāraḥ—ego; iti—thus; iyam—all these; me—my; bhinnā—divisions; prakṛitiḥ—material energy; aṣhṭadhā—eightfold
