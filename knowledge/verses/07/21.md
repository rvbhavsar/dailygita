# Chapter 7, Verse 21

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति।

तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम्।।7.21।।

## Transliteration

yo yo yāṁ yāṁ tanuṁ bhaktaḥ śhraddhayārchitum ichchhati
tasya tasyāchalāṁ śhraddhāṁ tām eva vidadhāmyaham

## Translations

- *Shri Purohit Swami*: But whatever form of worship, if the devotee has faith, then I set My own seal upon their faith in that worship.
- *Swami Adidevananda*: Whichever devotee seeks to worship with faith whatever form, I make their faith steadfast.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Whichever form of a deity any devotee wants to worship with faith, I strengthen that very firm faith of theirs.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Whatever form any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that same faith of his firm and unflinching.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Whatever form of deity a devotee, whomever they may be, desires to worship with faith, I assume that form which is firm and is according to their faith.

## Word meanings

yaḥ yaḥ—whoever; yām yām—whichever; tanum—form; bhaktaḥ—devotee; śhraddhayā—with faith; architum—to worship; ichchhati—desires; tasya tasya—to him; achalām—steady; śhraddhām—faith; tām—in that; eva—certainly; vidadhāmi—bestow; aham—I
