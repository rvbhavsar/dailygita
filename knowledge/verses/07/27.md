# Chapter 7, Verse 27

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत।

सर्वभूतानि संमोहं सर्गे यान्ति परन्तप।।7.27।।

## Transliteration

ichchhā-dveṣha-samutthena dvandva-mohena bhārata
sarva-bhūtāni sammohaṁ sarge yānti parantapa

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: O Arjuna, all beings are deluded as soon as they are born, due to the delusion of the pairs of opposites springing from desire and hate.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, O destroyer of foes, due to the delusion of duality arising from likes and dislikes, all creatures become bewildered upon their birth.
- *Swami Sivananda*: O Bharata, all beings are subject to delusion at birth due to the delusion of the pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Parantapa.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O descendant of Bharata, O scorcher of foes! At the time of creation, all beings become deluded due to the illusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and hatred.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: O brave Arjuna! Man lives in a fantastical world, deceived by the glamour of opposing sensations, infatuated by desire and aversion.

## Word meanings

ichchhā—desire; dveṣha—aversion; samutthena—arise from; dvandva—of duality; mohena—from the illusion; bhārata—Arjun, descendant of Bharat; sarva—all; bhūtāni—living beings; sammoham—into delusion; sarge—since birth; yānti—enter; parantapa—Arjun, conqueror of enemies

## Themes

Getting pulled into confusion and conflict by strong likes, dislikes, desire and hatred.

**Keywords:** likes, dislikes, love, hate, wanting, aversion, confused, torn, conflicted, biased, reactive, emotional
