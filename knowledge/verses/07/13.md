# Chapter 7, Verse 13

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत्।

मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम्।।7.13।।

## Transliteration

tribhir guṇa-mayair bhāvair ebhiḥ sarvam idaṁ jagat
mohitaṁ nābhijānāti māmebhyaḥ param avyayam

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The entire universe is deluded by these three states originating from the Gunas (of Prakrti), and fails to recognize Me, who am beyond them and immutable.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: All this world, deluded as it is by these three qualities made of the gunas, does not know Me, who am transcendental to these and undecaying.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Deluded by these Natures, composed of the three qualities of Nature, all this world does not know Me as distinct from them and immutable.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Being deluded by these three strands of nature, this entire world does not recognize Me, who am eternal and transcend these strands.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: The inhabitants of the world, misled by the natures engendered by the Qualities, do not know that I am higher than them all and that I do not change.

## Word meanings

tribhiḥ—by three; guṇa-mayaiḥ—consisting of the modes of material nature; bhāvaiḥ—states; ebhiḥ—all these; sarvam—whole; idam—this; jagat—universe; mohitam—deluded; na—not; abhijānāti—know; mām—me; ebhyaḥ—these; param—the supreme; avyayam—imperishable

## Themes

How being absorbed in shifting moods and surface appearances keeps you from recognizing a deeper reality.

**Keywords:** confusion, delusion, illusion, distraction, surface appearances, moods, bias, short-sightedness, being fooled, missing the bigger picture, clarity, seeing clearly, awareness, wakefulness
