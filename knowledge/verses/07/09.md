# Chapter 7, Verse 9

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ।

जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु तपश्चास्मि तपस्विषु।।7.9।।

## Transliteration

puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛithivyāṁ cha tejaśh chāsmi vibhāvasau
jīvanaṁ sarva-bhūteṣhu tapaśh chāsmi tapasviṣhu

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I am the pure scent in the earth; I am the radiance in the fire; I am the life-force in all beings, and austerity in ascetics.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: I am also the sweet fragrance in the earth; I am the brilliance in the fire, and the life in all beings; and I am the austerity of the ascetics.
- *Swami Sivananda*: I am the sweet fragrance in the earth and the brilliance in the fire, the life in all beings, and I am the austerity of ascetics.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: I am the pure scent in the earth; I am also the radiance in the sun; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in the ascetics.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: I am the fragrance of the earth, the brilliance of fire, the life force in all beings, and the austerity of ascetics.

## Word meanings

puṇyaḥ—pure; gandhaḥ—fragrance; pṛithivyām—of the earth; cha—and; tejaḥ—brilliance; cha—and; asmi—I am; vibhāvasau—in the fire; jīvanam—the life-force; sarva—in all; bhūteṣhu—beings; tapaḥ—penance; cha—and; asmi—I am; tapasviṣhu—of the ascetics
