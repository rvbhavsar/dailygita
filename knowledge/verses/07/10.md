# Chapter 7, Verse 10

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम्।

बुद्धिर्बुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम्।।7.10।।

## Transliteration

bījaṁ māṁ sarva-bhūtānāṁ viddhi pārtha sanātanam
buddhir buddhimatām asmi tejas tejasvinām aham

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Know Me, O Arjuna, to be the primeval seed of all beings. I am the intelligence of the discerning and the brilliance of the brilliant.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Partha, know Me to be the eternal seed of all beings. I am the intellect of the intelligent, and I am the courage of the courageous.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Know Me, O Arjuna, as the eternal seed of all beings; I am the intelligence of the intelligent, and the splendour of the splendid objects.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O son of Prtha! Know Me as the eternal seed of all beings; I am the intellect of the intellectuals and the brilliance of the brilliant.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Know, O Arjuna, that I am the eternal seed of being; I am the intelligence of the intelligent, the splendour of the resplendent.

## Word meanings

bījam—the seed; mām—me; sarva-bhūtānām—of all beings; viddhi—know; pārtha—Arjun, the son of Pritha; sanātanam—the eternal; buddhiḥ—intellect; buddhi-matām—of the intelligent; asmi—(I) am; tejaḥ—splendor; tejasvinām—of the splendid; aham—I

## Themes

Recognizing that intelligence, talent and greatness in all people grow from the same inner seed.

**Keywords:** potential, seed, growth, intelligence, wisdom, brilliance, talent, greatness, excellence, gifted, spark, inner, source
