# Chapter 7, Verse 26

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन।

भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन।।7.26।।

## Transliteration

vedāhaṁ samatītāni vartamānāni chārjuna
bhaviṣhyāṇi cha bhūtāni māṁ tu veda na kaśhchana

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: I know all beings, O Arjuna, past, present, and those to come; but no one knows Me.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: O Arjuna, I know the past, present, and future beings; but no one knows Me!
- *Swami Sivananda*: I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, the present, and the future; however, no one knows Me.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: O Arjuna, I know the beings that have gone, that are present, and those yet to be born; but no one knows Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: I know, O Arjuna, all beings in the past, the present, and the future; but they do not know Me.

## Word meanings

veda—know; aham—I; samatītāni—the past; vartamānāni—the present; cha—and; arjuna—Arjun; bhaviṣhyāṇi—the future; cha—also; bhūtāni—all living beings; mām—me; tu—but; veda—knows; na kaśhchana—no one
