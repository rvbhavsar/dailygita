# Chapter 7, Verse 22

Part of [Gyaan Vigyana Yoga](/chapters/07.md).

## Sanskrit

स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्याराधनमीहते।

लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान् हि तान्।।7.22।।

## Transliteration

sa tayā śhraddhayā yuktas tasyārādhanam īhate
labhate cha tataḥ kāmān mayaiva vihitān hi tān

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: Endowed with that faith, he worships that form and thereby gets the objects of his desire, granted in reality by Me alone.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Being imbued with that faith, that person engages in worshipping that form, and they get those very desired results as dispensed by Me alone.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that form and obtains his desired outcome, which is ordained by Me alone.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: Endowed with that faith, he seeks to worship that deity and thereby receives his desired objects, which are ordained by none other than Me.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: If he worships one form alone with real faith, then his desires shall be fulfilled through that alone; for thus I have ordained.

## Word meanings

saḥ—he; tayā—with that; śhraddhayā—faith; yuktaḥ—endowed with; tasya—of that; ārādhanam—worship; īhate—tries to engange in; labhate—obtains; cha—and; tataḥ—from that; kāmān—desires; mayā—by me; eva—alone; vihitān—granted; hi—certainly; tān—those

## Themes

Understanding that steady faith can bring the results you seek yet the deeper source of help lies beyond the specific form you focus on.

**Keywords:** faith, devotion, persistence, prayer, results, trust, hope, reward, effort, answered, help, receiving
