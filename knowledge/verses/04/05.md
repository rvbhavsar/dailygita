# Chapter 4, Verse 5

Part of [Jnana Karma Sanyasa Yoga](/chapters/04.md).

## Sanskrit

श्री भगवानुवाच

बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन।

तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप।।4.5।।

## Transliteration

śhrī bhagavān uvācha
bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava chārjuna
tānyahaṁ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṁ vettha parantapa

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: The Lord said, "Many of My births have passed, O Arjuna, as have yours. I know them all, but you do not know them."
- *Swami Gambirananda*: The Blessed Lord said, "O Arjuna, many lives of Mine have passed, and so have yours. I know them all, but you do not know, O scorcher of enemies!"
- *Swami Sivananda*: The Blessed Lord said, "Many births of Mine have passed, as well as of thine, O Arjuna; I know them all, but thou knowest not, O Parantapa (scorcher of foes)."
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: The Bhagavat said, "O Arjuna, many births of Mine as well as yours have passed. All of them I know, but you do not, O scorcher of foes!"
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: Lord Shri Krishna replied: I have been born again and again, from time to time; thou too, O Arjuna! My births are known to me, but yours are unknown to you.

## Word meanings

śhrī-bhagavān uvācha—the Supreme Lord said; bahūni—many; me—of mine; vyatītāni—have passed; janmāni—births; tava—of yours; cha—and; arjuna—Arjun; tāni—them; aham—I; veda—know; sarvāṇi—all; na—not; tvam—you; vettha—know; parantapa—Arjun, the scorcher of foes

## Themes

This verse is about realizing your life has a much longer journey than you currently remember or understand.

**Keywords:** forgetting, remembering, past, identity, continuity, perspective, awareness, journey, history, limited, self
