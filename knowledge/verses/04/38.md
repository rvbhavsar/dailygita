# Chapter 4, Verse 38

Part of [Jnana Karma Sanyasa Yoga](/chapters/04.md).

## Sanskrit

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते।

तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति।।4.38।।

## Transliteration

na hi jñānena sadṛiśhaṁ pavitramiha vidyate
tatsvayaṁ yogasansiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati

## Translations

- *Swami Adidevananda*: For there is no purifier equal to knowledge; he who is perfected in Karma Yoga finds this knowledge of his own accord in himself in due time.
- *Swami Gambirananda*: Indeed, there is nothing purifying here comparable to Knowledge. One who has become perfected over time through yoga, realizes That in their own heart.
- *Swami Sivananda*: Verily, there is no purifier in this world like knowledge. He who is perfected in Yoga finds it within the Self in due time.
- *Dr. S. Sankaranarayan*: In this world, there exists no purifier comparable to knowledge. One who becomes perfect in Yoga finds this, of their own accord, in their Self in due course.
- *Shri Purohit Swami*: There is nothing in the world so purifying as wisdom; and he who is a perfect saint finds that at last within himself.

## Word meanings

na—not; hi—certainly; jñānena—with divine knowledge; sadṛiśham—like; pavitram—pure; iha—in this world; vidyate—exists; tat—that; svayam—oneself; yoga—practice of yog; sansiddhaḥ—he who has attained perfection; kālena—in course of time; ātmani—wihtin the heart; vindati—finds

## Themes

Nothing cleanses the mind like genuine knowledge, which comes on its own through steady practice and patience.

**Keywords:** purification, clarity, patience, practice, growth, maturity, wisdom, discipline, awakening, stillness, timing, ripening
